# Product

## Register

product

## Users

CampusQA serves students, faculty, staff, and maintainers who need reliable answers from a campus knowledge base. They are usually in a focused task: checking policy, finding a document-backed answer, uploading reference material, or reviewing prior question history.

## Product Purpose

The product provides a ChatGPT-style knowledge-base assistant backed by FastAPI endpoints for chat, session history, uploads, mode switching, knowledge-base scans, and cost inspection. Success means users can ask, verify, revise, and continue conversations without losing context or wondering what system state they are in.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, capable. The interface should feel like a dependable academic operations tool rather than a marketing page or a toy chatbot.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative AI SaaS tropes: oversized hero layouts, purple-blue gradient surfaces, glass cards, animated spectacle, emoji-heavy controls, and repeated icon-card grids. Avoid hiding operational state behind tiny ambiguous controls.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the conversation primary, but expose system state clearly.
2. Make retrieval and knowledge-base operations feel operational and accountable.
3. Prefer familiar product controls over custom novelty.
4. Use density where it helps scanning, and whitespace where it protects reading.
5. Preserve keyboard-first chat ergonomics on desktop and mobile.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Preserve visible focus states, support reduced motion, avoid color-only status indicators, keep touch targets usable on mobile, and maintain readable Markdown output for long answers and code blocks.
