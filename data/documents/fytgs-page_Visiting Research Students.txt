  Visiting Research Students
  If you are currently studying in other institutions* and wish to do research study at HKUST(GZ) for a short period of time (one to six months), you may consider applying for enrolment as a Visiting Research Student. During your time at HKUST(GZ), you may extend your research period once if necessary. Besides gaining valuable research experience in a new environment, you may also enjoy a rewarding cultural exchange experience in Guangzhou, China.
  *Please note that Visiting Research Student status is not applicable to students from HKUST, as such mobility follows the established Cross-campus Studies mechanism. For undergraduate students from HKUST, please contact the Office of Global Learning at HKUST at
  study.abroad@ust.hk
  . For postgraduate students from HKUST and HKUST(GZ), please contact the Global Learning Department at HKUST(GZ) at
  ccsct@hkust-gz.edu.cn
  .
  Before you decide to apply, you are invited to browse the research output of our scholars:
  Research Areas @ HKUST(GZ)
  Eligibility
  To be eligible for Visiting Research Study at HKUST(GZ), you should:
  currently be a registered student at a recognized tertiary institution*
  have an educational background deemed satisfactory by the Hub & Thrust as a Visiting Research Student; and
  have gained agreement in advance from your home institution
  *International students shall observe and abide by the corresponding China student visa regulations.
  Application Procedures
  Step 1
  Identify the
  Thrust Area
  you are interested in
  Seek agreement from an
  HKUST(GZ) faculty member
  to accept you as a visiting research student
  Send "
  Support from Home University/ Institution
  " Form to your home institution to request for confirmation of support at HKUST(GZ) and upload to the application system
  Step 2
  Chinese students: submit an
  online application
  at least 2 months prior to the commencement date
  International students: submit an
  online application
  at least 4 months prior to the commencement date
  Step 3
  After getting an offer:
  Accept offer via the Online Admission System and
  Arrange official transcripts and other necessary documentation to be sent directly by the issuing authorities by the deadline stipulated in the offer letter/ Document Checklist.
  Step 4
  Apply for student visa at the local Chinese embassy/consulate (for international students)
  Step 5
  Register as a Visiting Research Student at HKUST(GZ) enrolling in a 1-credit project course
  Fees
  Please visit
  Fees

  Student Visa/Travel Permit
  All international students are required to enter China with the appropriate student visa. Considering the time needed for the international travel arrangement e.g. visa application, early application is highly recommended.
  Students from Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan regions are required to enter China’s mainland with Mainland Travel Permit for Hong Kong and Macau Residents and Mainland Travel Permit for Taiwan Residents, respectively.
  Financial Assistance
  Visiting Research Students are in general expected to have adequate financial means to support themselves. Allowance may be provided up to RMB 8000 per month (before tax), subject to the agreement of supervisor and the availability of funds.
  Accommodation
  Priority for university housing is given to the regular students. As such, please be prepared that you may have to arrange your own off-campus accommodation.
