content: >
  Selection of applications will be based on the following criteria:
  - Whether the paper has been referred or not
  - Whether the paper will be presented orally or not
  - The significance of the applicant's contribution to the paper
  - First-time applicants have priority over the awardees of DSA RTG in previous years.
  - The first PhD student authors have priority over the second PhD student authors. If the first
  PhD author already obtains financial support from the University or DSA, the 2nd author will
  be disqualified for DSA RTG.
