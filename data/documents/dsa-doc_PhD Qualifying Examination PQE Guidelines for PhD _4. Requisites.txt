content: >
  Before attempting the PhD qualifying examination, DSA PhD students must obtain a grade B
  or above in any two of the following PG courses in the DSA program:
  - DSAA 5002 Data Mining and Knowledge Discovery in Data Science (normally offered
  in the fall term)
  - DSAA 5009 Deep Learning in Data Science (normally offered in the spring term)
  - DSAA 5012 Advanced Database Management for Data Science (normally offered in the
  spring term)
