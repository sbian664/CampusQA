content: >
  The DSA GTA Guidelines take effect from July 16, 2026, with GTA arrangements governed
  herein formally implemented for the 2026-27 Fall Semester and afterwards. Students in the
  intake of 2026-27 Fall and afterward cohorts shall strictly follow the DSA GTA Guidelines to
  ensure high-quality teaching training.
  Students in the intakes of 2022-23 Fall to 2025-26 Spring should follow the requirements
  stipulated by RPG Handbook and University GTA Guidelines to complete 2 times of GTA
  duties in different terms. Students who haven't completed the 2 duties before or in 2025-26
  Summer should arrange the remaining GTA under management of DSA Thrust. Any service
  records without prior approval from DSA Thrust before the commencement of GTA duty will
  not be recognized as completion of Thrust specific GTA requirements.
  The final right of interpretation resides with DSA Thrust.
  Glossary:
  1. GTA: Graduate Teaching Assistants
  2. Courses managed by VPTL: course code starts with "UFUG", "UCUG", "UCMP",
  "LANG", "PLED", etc.
  3. Courses managed by Hub/Thrust: course code starts with "INFH", "DSAA", etc.
  4. Course Offering Units:
  DSA Thrust-course code starts with "DSAA";
  Information Hub- course code starts with "INFH";
  College of Education Science: course code starts with "UFUG", "UCUG", etc.
