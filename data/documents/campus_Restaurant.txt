Catesun Group Canteen

Location

Located on the ground floor of Building C6 on campus.

Canteen Setting

Features

Breakfast: Rice noodle rolls, congee, soybean milk, dim sum, noodles, whole grains, etc.

Lunch & Dinner: Mainly serving casual daily meals, featuring Cantonese, Sichuan, Hunan and Northeast Chinese dishes.

Specialty Dishes: Cantonese roast meats, Cantonese-style brined delicacies, Chaoshan soup noodles, fried rice, spicy hot pot.

Yilin Hong Kong Style Tea Restaurant

Location

Located on the second floor of Building C6 on campus.

Canteen Setting

Features

Yilin Hong Kong Style Tea Restaurant specializes in Hong Kong-style cuisine and beverages. It also serves Western light meals, signature Cantonese roast meats, rice with topping in Hong Kong Style, and stir-fried dishes. In addition, it serves a selection of handmade wontons, boiled dumplings, and Chinese hamburgers.
