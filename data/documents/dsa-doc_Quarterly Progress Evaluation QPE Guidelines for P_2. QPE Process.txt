content: >
  The first part is to be completed by the students who should provide a brief summary of
  research progress during the reporting period. The second part is an overall assessment given
  by the prime supervisor following discussions with the co-supervisor. If the assessment is
  unsatisfactory, comments and expectations must be provided. Students will be given the chance
  to view and discuss the result with their supervisors. The rating system is as below:
  Point Description
  5 Excellent progress
  4 Good progress
  3 Satisfactory progress
  2 Little progress (unsatisfactory)
  1 No progress (very unsatisfactory)
  If the supervisor decides to allocate an Unsatisfactory point (point 2/ point 1) to DSA PhD
  students, they should first send an email to the DSA Thrust (dsarpg@hkust-gz.edu.cn) for
  endorsement by the DSA PG Committee. Once the DSA PG Committee has provided their
  endorsement, the supervisor can then enter the evaluation result into the system for the student.
