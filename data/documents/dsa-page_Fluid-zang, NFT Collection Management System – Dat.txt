  Research Project
  Fluid-zang, NFT Collection Management System
  Abstract
  NFT, as a main application scenario on the blockchain, has attracted much attention in recent years. In this project, we have developed a digital collection management platform based on blockchain namely Fluid-zang, which is a collection management system that supports digitalizing the collections into NFT-based tokens on a blockchain system. Collectors and users can use Fluid-zang to achieve digitalized storage of collections, traceability management, and online collection trading.
