description: >
  Advances in machine learning and deep machine learning are continuously penetrating computational science and engineering. This course will introduce students to recent advances in data-driven scientific research based on deep learning techniques. It will cover topics including advanced deep neural network models, physics-informed neural networks (PINN) for model discovery, prediction, and uncertainty quantification, and other computational tasks (e.g., molecular dynamics simulation and deep potentials) aided by deep learning methods.
