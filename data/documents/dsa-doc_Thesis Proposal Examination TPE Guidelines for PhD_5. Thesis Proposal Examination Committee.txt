section: 5. Thesis Proposal Examination Committee
source_pdf: DSA-Thesis Proposal Examination (TPE) Guidelines-Version 202512.pdf
page_range: 1-2

content: >
  The TPE Committee must consist of a minimum of 4 faculty members: one chairperson, the
  prime supervisor, the co-supervisor, and one examiner.
  The majority (at least three members) of the TPE Committee members must be from the Thrust
  of Data Science and Analytics. The chairperson and the examiner must be substantiation-track
  faculty members at HKUST(GZ) or HKUST, and the chairperson must be at the rank of
  Associate Professor or above. Other than the supervisors, the membership of the TPE
  Committee is proposed by the student in consultation with the supervisors, and should be
  approved by the DSA PG Coordinator.
