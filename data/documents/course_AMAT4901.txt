description: >
  This is a course designed for the students who are in their final year of study. The course offers students the opportunity to undertake a year-long research project under the supervision of a faculty member in AMAT thrust. The findings will be presented in the form of a thesis. Credit load will be spread over a year. This course aims at providing student a pathway towards conducting research that synergizes with his or her self-interest, allowing the student to explore research under the guidance of a faculty member. Instructor’s approval is required for enrollment in this course.
