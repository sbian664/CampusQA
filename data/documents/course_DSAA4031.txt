description: >
  This course is an advanced course for understanding the importance of data quality (with an exposure of typical data quality topics such as missing values, outliers, (near-) duplicates and entity resolution) for different data science tasks (e.g., analytical queries, visualizations, and machine learning), and solving them using rule-based methods, machine learning based, deep learning based, and foundation model based methods. It will also introduce methods to improve machine/deep learning model performance throughout its life cycle.
