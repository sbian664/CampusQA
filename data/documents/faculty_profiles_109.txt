  研究方向：
    The Creative Intelligence and Synergy (CIS) Lab at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou) conducts cutting-edge research at the intersection of computer graphics, human-computer interaction, and artificial intelligence, with a focus on algorithms and systems for digital content creation. Our current research topics include sketching, VR/AR/XR, generative techniques, and multimodal creativity, with applications in art, design, perception, and cultural heritage.，
    • Drawing analysis and intelligent interfaces, including non-photorealistic rendering, sketch-based modeling, and AI-assisted drawing，
    • Virtual/augmented/extended reality, including efficient 3D capture, processing, and editing, spatial interfaces, and digital twin，
    • AI content generation, including neural rendering, digital humans, procedural modeling, and multimodal AI creativity，
    • Applications in art, design, perception, and cultural heritage, such as digitization of Longmen Grottoes and Dunhuang dance，
