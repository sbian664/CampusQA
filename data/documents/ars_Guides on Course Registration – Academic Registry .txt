  Course Enrollment
  Class enrollment of undergraduate and postgraduate students is processed with the web-based Student Information System (SIS). The main system functions include the following:
  Students can view their own class timetable.
  Students can submit class choices, confirm their places or/and make changes before the add/drop deadline.
  In case of over-subscription of classes, they can be put on the system-automated waiting list for class space.
  In case students require special approval from the faculty member/academic department for enrollment, requests can be submitted through the system.
  Students are welcome to contact their College/Hub and program department to seek academic advice whenever in need. In preparing the individual study plan, students can refer to online information such as Program and Course catalog (
  UG
  &
  PG
  ) for reference.
  Please note that
  students are responsible for their own studies. It is important for students to plan ahead to ensure they can complete the study within the permitted duration of study.
