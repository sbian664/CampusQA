  CreateLink Graph Data joint-lab, committed to building a world-class innovation hub in the field of graph technology. Graph technology is a fundamental key technique for conducting data association analysis and leveraging the network effects of data value. The laboratory possesses rich scientific and educational resources, as well as graph database platforms and infrastructure. It focuses on researching trillion-scale graph neural network computational frameworks, time-series graph processing for massive data, and transaction and performance optimization for distributed native graph databases, offering students the opportunity to deeply study cutting-edge graph technologies.
