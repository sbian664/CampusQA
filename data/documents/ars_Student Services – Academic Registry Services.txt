  Forms
  Titles with * are applied via e-doc system, electronic version will be provided which can be verified and viewed at HKUST(GZ) official website on Electronic Documents Verification Program:
  https://edoc.hkust-gz.edu.cn/home
  .
  Titles with # are applied via E-Form platform.
  All
  Program Registration
  Course
  Exam & Grade
  Graduation
  Testimonial *
  A testimonial is a certification of the student’s current registration status at the University. This application form is NOT applicable to students who have completed the program of study.
  Official Transcript*
  The academic transcript provides a FULL record of a student’s academic results and performance. This record may not be released to any other party without the written consent of the student.
  Class Enrollment/Withdrawal after Add/Drop Period#
  Requests to add a course, to withdraw from a course, to change course enrollment status, or to swap within a course after the add/drop period will not be accepted except under extenuating circumstances.
  Credit Transfer (PG)#
  Transfer of credits may be granted to students in recognition of studies completed elsewhere other than HKUST(GZ) at an appropriate level as recognized by the University.
  Credit Transfer (UG)#
  Transfer of credits may be granted to students in recognition of studies completed elsewhere other than HKUST and HKUST(GZ) at an appropriate level as recognized by the University.
  Program Transfer/Change of Academic Load#
  Application for program transfer and change of academic load should be submitted before the term the transfer takes effect.
  Leave from Study#
  The Leave from Study should be on term basis. Application for Leave from Study should be made prior to the beginning of a regular term, in which the leave from study status will become effective. Under exceptional circumstances, such as health issues, the leave period can commence at other times.
  Course Substitution/Deviation from Curriculum (PG)#
  This form is for students who wish to apply for course substitution or deviation from curriculum.
