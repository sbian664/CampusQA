content: >
  An internal GTA recruitment will be initiated by DSA Thrust via email around 6 weeks before
  the upcoming semesters.
  3.1 Internal Recruitment
  Round 1:
  During the 1st week of internal recruitment, instructors and task coordinators can extend direct
  invitations to selected students and report the mutual agreement between instructor-GTA to
  DSA Thrust.
  Round 2:
  From the 2nd week of internal recruitment, DSA Thrust collects the GTA vacancy information
  from the instructors and task coordinators and sends recruitment emails to all DSA full-time
  PhD students. A recruitment email will include headcounts, specific requirements, required
  qualifications, duty descriptions, and so on.
  Round 3:
  For any vacancies still unfilled after the above two rounds, DSA Thrust will assign eligible
  students to the positions based on faculty's recommendations and students' skill sets. When
  assigning service duties, DSA Thrust will consider each student's technical skill set and prior
  service record to distribute tasks reasonably and maintain balanced workloads across all PhD
  students.
  If all GTA openings managed by DSA Thrust are fully filled upon completion of the first two
  rounds, DSA Thrust will issue a Thrust-wide official email listing all preliminarily confirmed
  GTA assignments.
  3.2 Official Appointment for In-Course GTAs
  All in-course GTAs will be invited to submit applications in the GTA Management System in
  due course. The selection, appointment, and evaluation procedures will be completed in the
  System. The GTA responsibilities, workload, and other requirements follow the HKUST(GZ)
  GTA Management Guidelines.
