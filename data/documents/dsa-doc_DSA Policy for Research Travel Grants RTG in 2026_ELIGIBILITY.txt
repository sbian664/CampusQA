content: >
  Targeting students:
  1. the full-time students under PhD (DSA) or PhD (IIP-DSA) programs.
  2. the UG students under BSc (DSBD) or in Year 1-2 who have made outstanding research progress
  under the supervision of DSA faculty.
  Minimum Requirements:
  1. The conference must be listed in the selected list of conferences for DSA RTG (see Annex 1).
  If the event is not a conference, but a workshop, forum, competition or other, the applicant
  must provide a Special Request Letter with a brief introduction to the event together with the
  application materials.
  2. The research being presented must have been conducted at HKUST(GZ), and the first
  affiliation of the papers used to support PhD applications must be HKUST(GZ).
  3. Each paper is eligible to support only ONE UG student and/or ONE PhD student. There is no
  restriction on the order of authors for the UG applicant, while the PhD applicant must be the
  first or second author of papers accepted by the main conference, not the associated workshops
  or demo sessions.
  Note:
  1. Applicants must be registered at HKUST(GZ) or in the Pilot Scheme students when application.
  If the applicant will leave the University when the event is held, he/she is still eligible to apply
  for RTG if "HKUST(GZ)" is the affiliation of his/her paper to be presented at the event.
  2. Any deviation from the requirements above should be reviewed and approved by the DSA
  Research Support Committee on a case-by-case basis.
  3. DSA RTG is stackable with HKUST(GZ) RTG or the financial support from supervisors,
  however, all expenses must be compiled in a claim.
