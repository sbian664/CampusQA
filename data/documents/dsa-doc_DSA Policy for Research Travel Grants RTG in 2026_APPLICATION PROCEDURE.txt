content: >
  Applicants must send the following supporting documentation and information to dsa-
  research@hkust-gz.edu.cn via email, copying to their prime supervisors, at least 3 weeks before
  the start date of the event:
  a) Leave approvals (UG: email approval; PhD: Leave Form as a PDF file).
  b) The letter of acceptance of the publication.
  c) The invitation to present the paper stating the mode of presentation (oral vs. poster).
  d) The official link of paper to be presented, or the camera-ready version as a PDF file.
  e) The receipt of registration fee and the link of rates webpage of the event.
  f) Hotel reservation note.
  g) A brief budget plan (in the application email body).
  Budget Plan Template
  Items Amount Remarks
  e.g.: Registration fee USD 400 Register as 1st author
  e.g.: Hotel – 4 nights CNY 2,000 XXX Hotel
  e.g.: Flight ticket CNY 1,500 Economy Class
  h) The justification for choosing luxury hotels and others (if applicable, in the email body).
  i) The Special Request Letter for non-conference events (if applicable, in the email body).
  The result will be released to applicants at least 1 week prior to the event. Failure to submit the
  applications by the deadline will result in disqualification. Post-event applications will not be
  entertained.
