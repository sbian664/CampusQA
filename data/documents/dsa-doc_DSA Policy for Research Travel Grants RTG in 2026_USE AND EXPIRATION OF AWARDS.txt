content: >
  The DSA RTG can be used only for the purpose for which they were awarded and are not
  transferable for other trips. Awardees are encouraged to use DSA RTG specifically for the
  reimbursement of registration fees, rather than for other travel expenses.
  Awardees should submit the DSA RTG Report (find template in the Annex II) to dsa-
  research@hkust-gz.edu.cn within 1 week from the end date of the event. Late report submissions
  may cause disqualification.
  Once the report is endorsed by DSA Thrust, the applicant will be granted access to DSA Thrust fund.
  All reimbursement files (see reimbursement guide in the folder "Student Reimbursement" in the
  DSA Intra SharePoint) must be submitted in PBMS – "Student Travel" within 1 week after the
  applicant is granted the DSA Thrust fund.
  - Select "Non-RTG" when the awardee is supported by DSA RTG and/or supervisors' funds.
  - Select "RTG" when the awardee is receiving both HKUST(GZ) RTG and DSA RTG, and/or
  supervisors' support.
  Awardees are also required to share their experiences gained from attending the conference in
  various student activities which include, but are not limited to, the DSA Academic Salon, DSA
  Poster Pop-Up, and other related events.
  Note: For events scheduled between November 20th and December 31st, the deadline for
  reimbursement materials submissions will be determined based on the HKUST(GZ) Financial
  Department's fiscal year-end closing schedule and must be submitted within 3 days from the end
  date of the event. For events scheduled at the very end of the year, applicants should follow the
  notifications sent from Finance Department and DSA Thrust.
