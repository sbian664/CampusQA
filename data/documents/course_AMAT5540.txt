description: >
  This course emphasizes the critical role of nanotechnology in the modern technology, focusing on the distinctive properties of low-dimensional nanomaterials and nanostructures across one to three dimensions. It encompasses their classification, fabrication methods, and varied applications in photonics, sensors, catalysis, energy storage, etc. By offering a comprehensive overview, the course will help students to conduct research in nanotechnology.
