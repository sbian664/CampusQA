We will examine state-of-the-art defense strategies, including robust training techniques, cryptographic protections, and privacy-preserving frameworks, while addressing ethical, regulatory, and practical challenges. By the end of the course, students will be equipped to identify, analyze, and mitigate privacy and security risks in foundation models, balancing innovation with responsible deployment.
