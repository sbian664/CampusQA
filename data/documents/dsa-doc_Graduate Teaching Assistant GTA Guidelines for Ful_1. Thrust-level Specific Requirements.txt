content: >
  Per HKUST(GZ) RPG Handbook, all full-time PhD students must fulfill Graduate Teaching
  Assistant (GTA) duties as part of graduation requirements. DSA full-time PhD students are
  required to complete at least 3 times and at most 6 times of GTA services, in the frequence of
  one time in maximum each semester, under the arrangement of DSA Thrust during their
  normative PhD study period. The service period starts usually from the 2nd semester of Year 1
  (included) and will normally end in the first semester of normative study or the semester of
  PhD Thesis Examination (included). Among the duties above, at least one semester of service
  must be in the university-level teaching activities (VPTL courses), upon approval from DSA
  Thrust.
  Students shall be assigned one of the following two categories of duties, with equivalent
  workload and hourly requirements (5 hours per week and 70 hours per semester) applicable to
  both categories:
  Category 1: In-course GTA duties tied to specific courses taught or coordinated by DSA
  Thrust/Faculty; the full lifecycle will be captured within the GTA Management System;
  Category 2: Academic special tasks, including Thrust academic operations and events. As such
  tasks are not attached to any individual course, they will not be entered into the GTA
  Management System but be retained within internal Thrust records.
