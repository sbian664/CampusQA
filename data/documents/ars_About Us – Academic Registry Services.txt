  Academic Registry Services
  Underpin the Academic Excellence and Support Students' Academic Journey
  The Department of Academic Registry Services serves as a central administrative unit within the University, responsible for managing student records, enrollment, academic processes and related services to students, faculty, and staff in various administrative areas. The department now consists of four specialized teams dedicated to program registration, class scheduling, exam and grade management, and graduation management. These teams work collaboratively to support students and faculty in navigating the academic landscape, providing essential services and guidance to facilitate a seamless academic experience. The department is committed to maintaining the integrity of student records, promoting academic excellence, and fostering a supportive environment for all members of the university community.
