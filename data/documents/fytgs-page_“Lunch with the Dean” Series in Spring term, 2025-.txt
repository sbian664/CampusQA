text: >
  40 postgraduate (PG) students attended two lunches on Mar 16 and Mar 17, 2026. Participants came from all four Hubs and the RBM, both research and taught postgraduate programs, different cultural backgrounds, and from many geographical regions.
  The lunch series provided a unique opportunity for students to connect not only with the Acting Dean and the FYTGS staff, but also with their fellow PG students. Students shared their academic aspirations and learnt from one another’s experiences, making it a truly enriching gathering.
  The “Lunch with the Dean” Series will recommence in the Fall term, 2026-27. Stay tuned for more details! We look forward to seeing you there!
