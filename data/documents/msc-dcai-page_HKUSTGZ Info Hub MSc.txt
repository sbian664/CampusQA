  General Information on MSc DCAI
  Master of Science in Data-Centric Artificial Intelligence Technology (MSc DCAI)
  Full-time and Part-time
  » Duration
  Full-time: 2 yrs
  Part-time: 3 yrs
  » Total no. of credits
  39 credits/program
  » Medium of Instruction
  English
  Face-to-face mode of delivery
  (*Some courses may be delivered in mixed mode to allow in-need students to attend the courses
  via real-time online mode.)
  Degree awarded
  Master of Science (MSc), The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology
  Program Features
  The MSc in Data-Centric Artificial Intelligence Technology (MSc DCAI) program at the Hong Kong
  University of Science and
  Technology (Guangzhou) is
  the first MSc program in Asia to implement a dual
  supervision system and to provide each student with a year-long internship in industry.
  A year-long internship
  at a variety of prestigious
  organizations or companies around the world, such as Alibaba, Huawei, MSRA, Samsung, NAVER, etc.
  Dual supervision system
  allows each student to have an
  academic supervisor from HKUST & HKUST (GZ), and an industrial supervisor including vice
  presidents, chief scientists, and directors of leading technology enterprises.
  Program Objectives
  Educate students with academic literacy in
  big data and AI.
  Provide students with hands-on experience to work on
  industry-level independent projects and
  internships
  under the guidance of technology leaders from leading enterprises worldwide.
  Enables students to apply learning to practice, empowering students to be skilled
  technology
  leaders and successful big data and AI professionals
  in the fast-changing business environment.
  Intended Learning Outcomes
  Develop advanced knowledge and understanding of
  state-of-the-art big data and AI technologies
  and analysis methods
  , such as Data Mining, AI/ Deep Learning, Data Science and Engineering
  (Database, Hadoop, HDFS, MLOps), Algorithms, Probabilistic Graphical Models (GNN).
  Perform various industry
  data analytics tasks
  using big data, AI, and computing techniques.
  Exercise independent thinking and demonstrate critical analytical skills essential from
  the
  perspective of big data and AI
  .
  Investigate existing problems in big data and AI and conduct original big data and AI research
  independently with in-depth knowledge and practical experience to
  solve complex problems in the
  industry
  .
  Apply a range of big data and AI knowledge and techniques effectively into
  practice in the
  academic field and industry
  for robust data analytics and applications.
