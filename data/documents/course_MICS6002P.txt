description: >
  Building on semiconductor fundamentals and device physics, this course explores the latest developments in advanced logic and memory devices, covering nanoscale CMOS transistor scaling, short-channel effects, advanced process technologies, and reliability challenges. Beyond logic devices, it offers a comprehensive overview of memory technologies, including volatile memories (SRAM, DRAM) and non-volatile memories (Flash, ReRAM, ferroelectric memory), emphasizing their device physics, charge or polarization mechanisms, performance trade-offs, and integration with logic circuits. Through comparative analysis, the course highlights how emerging memory technologies complement and extend CMOS logic to enable next-generation computing architectures.
