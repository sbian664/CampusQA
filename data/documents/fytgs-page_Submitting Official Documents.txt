  Documents Checklist
  Please prepare your documents for qualification verification referring to your checklist in the
  Online Admission System
  and the specific requirements below.
  NOTE:
  If the original documents are NOT in English or Chinese,
  please send us the notarized English or Chinese translation. We understand that the notary/ governmental authority may not provide the direct delivery service to the third party, you can mail the documents to us.
  Degree Certificate
  Please provide your degree certificate or letter of graduation certification according to your state of graduation as follows:
  Degree Certificate
  (for graduates)
  Degree Certificate should be provided by a
  certified true copy of the degree certificate/ diploma.
  (NOT required for submission if the degree award is explicitly specified in the transcript)
  Alternatively, applicants from China’s mainland can provide Online Verification Report of Higher Education Degree Certificate (中国高等教育学位在线验证报告) issued by the
  CHSI (中国高等教育学生信息网)
  .
  Letter of graduation certification
  (for final year student)
  Final year students with confirmed degree awards, but without available degree certificates/diplomas before HKUST(GZ) commences, may submit a graduation certification letter as proof. The letter should be issued by a central unit (e.g. Registry or equivalent) of your home institution.
  The letter should include the following:
  • Your name and identification information (e.g. date of birth, ID no., etc)
  • The degree you will be awarded
  • The date of conferment and (if applicable) award classification
  • Letterhead of your home institution
  • The name and position of the person authorized to issue the certificate
  • Official stamp of your home institution
  NOTE：For degrees from countries that do not follow the bachelor-master-doctoral system
  , diploma supplement or other official document showing that the degree is at, or above bachelor’s degree level is required for submission.
  Official Transcripts
  Transcripts should be issued by a central unit (e.g. Registry or equivalent) of your home institution.
  Alternatively, applicants from China’s mainland can provide Verification Report of China Higher Education Student’s Academic Transcript (中国高等学校学生成绩验证报告) issued by
  CHSI (中国高等教育学生信息网)
  .
  Grading System of Home Institution
  The explanation of the grading system is normally included on your final transcript. If not, please provide
  a letter of GPA certification
  issued by a central unit of your home institution (e.g. Registry or equivalent).
  Alternatively, you can calculate your final GPA/grade/score and request your home institution to certify with official stamp and signature that it is accurate and complete.
  Proof of English Proficiency
  Please be advised to
  arrange electronic score reports
  sent directly by the issuing authority, and make sure that the qualified scores to be sent
  have been entered
  in the system before submitting the scores.
  IELTS
  • Name of institution:
  The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou)
  • Name of department: Other departments(E results required Duxue Road Nansha District)
  TOEFL
  • DI Code: D315
  Note:
  1. You are not required to take the English Proficiency Test if you obtained a bachelor's degree (equivalent or above) from an institution where the medium of instruction was English.
  2. As issuing authorities will not send reports for expired scores, it is important to arrange official reports to be sent
  before the test score expires
  , even if an admission offer has not been made.
  Acceptable Means for Submission
  Please provide your documents using one of the following means by the deadline stipulated in the offer letter.
  ATTENTION:
  Do NOT send us irreplaceable originals
  . Please note that all documents submitted will NOT be returned.
  Electronic Materials (most preferrable)
  Please send the official electronic documents to
  pgdocgz@hkust-gz.edu.cn
  quoting your application number and applied program,
  directly by the central unit
  (e.g. Registry, Archives Department or equivalent) of your institution,
  or via designated secure websites
  (e.g.
  CHSI (中国高等教育学生信息网)
  ,
  CSCSE (教育部留服认证)
  , 可信电子成绩单, Gradintelligence, My eQuals, etc.)
  .
  Hardcopy Materials

  an official hard copy*

  ii. hard copy in an envelope sealed and stamped by the institution (acceptable only if the issuing authority does not provide direct delivery service)

  Tips
  After submitting the documents, please check the most up-to-date document receipt status at the document checklist in the Online Admission System and take note of our remarks.
  The document verification process may last long (around 10 working days) due to the excessive workload. If the document receipt status is not updated 7 days after the expected receipt date, please double check with the issuing authority or courier service provider and ensure that the mailing address is correct.
