  Student Information System
  The Student Information System (SIS) enables students to:
  Maintain and view academic records
  Access and update personal particulars
  Enroll in classes
  Consult class and examination timetables
  Check if there is any outstanding fees to be settled
  View report showing their academic progress
  Submit the Notice of Intention Form to take Thesis Examination

  Thesis Examination System
  The Thesis Examination System is designed to support the following functions:
  Workflow from submission of the Notice of Intention Form to take Thesis Examination from the students to approval from Thrust, Hub and FYTGS(GZ)
  Maintenance of the Notice of Intention Form to take Thesis Examination
  Sending notifications (including scheduled reminders) to examination panel members, administrators and students
  Auto-posting of the thesis examination details to the University Calendar
  Queries and data download functions
