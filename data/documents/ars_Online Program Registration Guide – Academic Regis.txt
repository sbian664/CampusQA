  Online Program Registration
  Dear Students,
  Welcome to the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou)!
  As a new student, you will be invited to complete the Online Program Registration, an official email with subject
  “HKUST (GZ): Invitation for Program Registration” (Student No. XXXXXXX)
  will be sent to both of your personal email address and Campus Account (University Email Address) by the Academic Registry Services.
  Please read through the guidance on the email and
  complete your Online Program Registration before the deadline
  as stated in the email. Each student admitted to the University in a particular academic year must complete the online registration in their programs.
  More detailed guidance on completing your Online Program Registration can be found
  here
  .
  For any queries, please contact Academic Registry Services by sending email to
  gzars@hkust-gz.edu.cn
  .
  Academic Registry Services
  The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou)
