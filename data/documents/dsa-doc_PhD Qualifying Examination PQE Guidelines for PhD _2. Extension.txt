section: 2. Extension
source_pdf: DSA-PhD Qualifying Examination (PQE) Guidelines-Version 202512 (1).pdf
page_range: 1

content: >
  In exceptional cases where students require an extension of the University timeline (Full-time
  PhD students: 30 months after the commencement of their PhD program; Part-time PhD
  students: 60 months after the commencement of their PhD program), for example due to
  medical reasons, they are required to submit the request to the Committee on Postgraduate
  Studies for consideration of approval prior to exceeding the designated timeline. The request
  should be supported by the respective Thrusts and Hubs.
