text: >
  Congratulations on receiving your offer! Accept the offer now and get prepared for your study journey at HKUST(GZ)!
  Step 1 Accept the Admission Offer Online
  Confirm the offer and pay the first payment to confirm your acceptance.
  Tip: Offers are only considered accepted when both online acceptance and first payment are completed.
  Step 2 Begin the Student Visa Application (for International Students)
  Apply for the Student Visa (X visa) upon receiving the paper admission notice and JW 202/201 Form. All international students are required to obtain a student visa from the local Chinese embassy or consulate to enter China.
  Learn more
  Step 3 Submit Documents to Verify Your Qualification
  Arrange official transcripts and other necessary documentation to be sent directly by the issuing authorities by the deadline stipulated in the offer letter/ Document Checklist.
  Learn more
  Step 4 Program Registration with HKUST(GZ)
  Check out the invitation for program registration and complete it according to the instructions. It is expected to be conducted in mid-August for Fall entry or mid-January for Spring entry.
  Step 5 On-campus Registration and New Students Welcome Week at HKUST(GZ)
  Check out the invitation for on-campus registration and arrive at the campus on time with the required documents according to the instructions. All students shall complete the on-campus registration and attend the Welcome Week as announced.

  armd@hkust-gz.edu.cn
