  Policies, Procedures, Rules & Regulations
  As a condition of online program registration, students must agree to abide by the University’s policies, procedures, rules and regulations, as approved by the University from time to time, which include but are not limited to the following:
  Academic Honor Code
  Academic Regulations Governing Undergraduate Studies
  Academic Regulations Governing Postgraduate Studies
  Regulations for Student Conduct
  Regulations for Student Academic Integrity
  Examination regulations & guidelines (student)
  Supplementary Implementation Rules for Regulations for Student Academic Integrity & Regulations for Student Conduct
  Equal Opportunities and Anti-discrimination Policy
  Intellectual Property Policy
  IT Acceptable Use Policy
  Policy and Guidelines on the Use of the University’s Titles, Names and Logos:
  Policy
  Guidelines
  Policy on Research Conduct and Integrity
  Individual colleges, hubs, offices and departments may maintain policies for specific purposes.
