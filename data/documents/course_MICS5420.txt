  This course introduces the design and analysis of clock generation integrated circuits. The covered topics include basic concepts, basic oscillator, oscillator analysis, advanced oscillator techniques, basic phase-locked loop architecture, integer-N PLLs, fractional-N PLLs, frequency dividers and multipliers, digital PLLs, advanced frequency synthesis and clock data recovery.
