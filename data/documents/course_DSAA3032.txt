description: >
  This course introduces the fundamentals of high-performance and parallel computing. It targets at scientists, engineers, scholars, and everyone seeking to develop the software skills necessary for work in parallel software environments. These skills include big-data analysis, machine learning, parallel programming, and optimization. The course covers the basics of Linux environments and bash scripting all the way to high throughput computing and parallelizing code.
