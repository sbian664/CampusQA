section: 6. PhD Qualifying Examination Committee
source_pdf: DSA-PhD Qualifying Examination (PQE) Guidelines-Version 202512 (1).pdf
page_range: 2-3

content: >
  The PQE Committee must consist of a minimum of 4 faculty members: one chairperson, the
  prime supervisor, the co-supervisor, and one examiner.
  The majority (at least three members) of the PQE Committee members must be from the
  Thrust of Data Science and Analytics. Thechairperson and the examiner must be
  substantiation-track faculty members at HKUST(GZ) or HKUST, and the chairperson must be
  at the rank of Associate Professor or above. Other than the supervisors, the membership of the
  PQE Committee is proposed by the student in consultation with the supervisors, and should be
  approved by the DSA PG Coordinator.
