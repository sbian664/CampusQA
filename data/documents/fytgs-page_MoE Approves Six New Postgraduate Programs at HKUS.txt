  The Ministry of Education (MoE) has approved the following Research and Taught Postgraduate programs in March 2025.
  Research Programs:
  - MPhil/PhD in Carbon Neutrality and Climate Chang（哲学硕士/哲学博士（碳中和与气候变化））
  Taught Programs:
  - Master of Fine Arts in Computational Arts（文学/艺术硕士（计算艺术））
  - Master of Science in Smart Manufacturing（理学硕士 （智能制造））
  - Master of Science in Carbon Neutrality and Green Finance（理学硕士（碳中和与绿色金融））
  - Master of Business Administration in Innovation and Entrepreneurship（工商管理硕士（创新创业））
