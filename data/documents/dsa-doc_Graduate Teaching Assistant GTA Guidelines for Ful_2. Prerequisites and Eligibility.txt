section: 2. Prerequisites and Eligibility
source_pdf: DSA Internal GTA Guidlines-202607(1).pdf

content: >
  DSA full-time PhD students are required to fulfill the following requisites simultaneously
  within the first semester of Year 1:
  - Received a Pass grade in PDEV 6800Y Introduction to Teaching and Learning in Higher
  Education (10 hours training).
  - Received a Pass grade in the English Language Proficiency Assessment (ELPA) or in
  LANG 5000 Foundation in Listening and Speaking for Postgraduate Students.
